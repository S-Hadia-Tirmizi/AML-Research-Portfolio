# Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML)

### Molecular Biology | Bioinformatics | Biostatistics | Computational Drug Discovery

Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) is a heterogeneous hematological malignancy characterized by molecular alterations that affect myeloid differentiation, proliferation, and disease progression.

This repository brings together AML-focused research projects in molecular biology, transcriptomics, biostatistics, and computational drug discovery. It documents the scientific questions, analytical approaches, and computational workflows used to study AML.

## Areas of Focus

### 01. Molecular Biology of AML

* Molecular mechanisms of leukemogenesis
* Genetic mutations and molecular subtypes
* Dysregulated signaling pathways
* Mechanisms of treatment resistance

### 02. AML Bioinformatics

* Gene expression analysis
* Differential expression
* Functional enrichment and pathway analysis
* Gene expression patterns and molecular signatures

### 03. AML Biostatistics

* Survival analysis
* Cox proportional hazards regression
* Biomarker evaluation
* Statistical modeling and visualization

### 04. Computational Drug Discovery in AML

* Therapeutic target investigation
* Protein structure analysis
* Molecular docking
* Drug-likeness and ADMET evaluation
* Molecular dynamics

### 05. AML Research Methods

* Experimental design
* Sample size estimation
* Statistical analysis using R and Python
* Reproducible research workflows

## Research Projects

Independent AML-related projects and analytical workflows will be added as they are developed.

Each project will include its biological rationale, data source, methodology, analysis, findings, and limitations.

## Research Integrity

Publicly available datasets and independently developed workflows will be used where appropriate. Unpublished research, restricted data, and confidential materials are excluded from this repository.
